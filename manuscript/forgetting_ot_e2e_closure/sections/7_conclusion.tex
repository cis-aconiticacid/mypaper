\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Batched Sinkhorn exposes parallel work across independent couplings. DrainSinkhorn retains that joint execution for unfinished problems while combining Sinkhorn-specific screening, verification, and compaction to avoid later updates to verified-complete problems. Application experiments measure the resulting solver, pipeline, and training gains. Competitive MetroPT-3 controls show that indexed execution and in-place skipping achieve similar runtime to full-state compaction. Direct modifications of POT's native batch loop and GeomLoss's residual-refined loop further demonstrate acceleration within third-party solvers, with scale-dependent gains and explicit state-management costs. Independently calibrated costs reconstruct solver time on held-out target groups from their observed completion traces. These results establish completion-aware execution as a portable way to reduce repeated OT computation.
